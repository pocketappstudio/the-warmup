The WarmUp privacy policy

The WarmUp, made by pocketappstudio. Last updated: October 18, 2026.

The short version

The WarmUp collects no personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device, in your browser's storage for this site.

What the app stores on your device

Your inventory answers, growth areas, notes about your wife, pressures, current-need notes, prayers log, daily log and your license key are saved only on your device. The app does not upload them to pocketappstudio or anyone else. We run no server that receives your data.

Offline copy

To work without a signal, the app saves its own files (pages, scripts, images and the voice recordings) in your browser's cache on your device. This holds no personal information.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics, tracking, cookies, crash reporting or advertising IDs.
  • No selling or sharing of data.

The HEATED?!? page

Nothing you do on the HEATED?!? page is sent anywhere. It saves one small setting on your device: which tip and exercise it showed last, so they rotate. The voice recordings are files inside the app and play on your device. The voice is AI-generated.

Purchases and the license check

The full-access unlock is a one-time purchase sold through Gumroad. When you enter a license key, the key and the product ID are sent to Gumroad to confirm your purchase. Nothing else is sent. Gumroad's own privacy policy applies to that exchange and to your purchase. The web host, GitHub Pages, may log visitor IP addresses under GitHub's own policy, which we do not control.

Sensitive notes and your device

What you write about your wife and your life is not encrypted by the app. Anyone who can use your phone or browser can see it. Use a device passcode.

Backups and deleting your data

On the More screen you can export a backup file and erase everything. Clearing your browser's site data, or losing your device, also deletes your data. We keep no copy, so deleted data cannot be recovered.

Adults only

The WarmUp is for adults 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
