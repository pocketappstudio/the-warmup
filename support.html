The WarmUp support

The WarmUp, made by pocketappstudio. Last updated: October 18, 2026.

Getting help

For questions, bugs or feedback, open an issue on our public support page: github.com/pocketappstudio/the-warmup/issues. A free GitHub account is needed to post, and posts are public. Please do not post personal details or anything private about your family.

Purchases and refunds

  • iPhone app: request a refund through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. To restore your purchase, open More, then Unlock, then Restore Purchases.
  • Web version: purchases, receipts and refunds are handled through Gumroad. Use the receipt from your purchase.

Your data

Your data lives only on your device. On the More screen, use Export backup to keep a copy, or Erase everything to remove it.

If anyone is in danger

Step away, go somewhere safe, and call 911 (or your local emergency number). National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788. For crisis support, call or text 988.
